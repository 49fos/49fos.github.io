About INDOPARK

Company Profile

Two Decades of Industrial Leadership

Founded in 2005 under the strategic vision of Indonesia's industrial transformation, INDOPARK has evolved from a 400-hectare pilot zone into the nation's most expansive integrated industrial estate spanning 2,400 hectares across North Maluku.

Our estate hosts over 180 multinational and domestic companies spanning nickel smelting, stainless steel, ferronickel, chemical processing, and light manufacturing — anchored by world-class infrastructure purpose-built for heavy industrial operations.

INDOPARK operates through five subsidiaries covering land development, power generation, port management, water utilities, and worker township services — providing a fully integrated, one-stop industrial ecosystem.

Our Direction

Vision & Mission

🔭

Our Vision

To be Southeast Asia's most sustainable, technologically advanced, and globally connected integrated industrial park — enabling Indonesia to become a leading hub for value-added manufacturing and mineral processing by 2035.

🎯

Our Mission

To develop and operate world-class industrial infrastructure that accelerates Indonesia's economic growth, creates meaningful employment opportunities, attracts sustainable foreign direct investment, and upholds the highest standards of environmental stewardship and community development.
