About INDOPARK

Indonesia's Gateway to Industrial Growth

Established in 2005, INDOPARK has grown into the nation's most strategically positioned industrial estate — offering plug-and-play facilities, deep-sea port access, and a supportive regulatory environment.

Our 2,400-hectare integrated complex spans manufacturing zones, nickel smelting facilities, energy infrastructure, and a fully serviced township for workers and management.
